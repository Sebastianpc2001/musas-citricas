# Musas Cítricas - Brand Notes

## Working intent
Musas Cítricas should feel vivid, feminine, editorial, handmade, and culturally warm without becoming visually noisy. The brand should feel like a curated citrus dream: bright, poetic, textural, and playful, but still premium.

## Visual direction
- Mood: citrus, sun, skin, freshness, poetry, intimate, artisanal, soft boldness
- Tone: expressive, feminine, magnetic, warm, romantic, slightly surreal
- Avoid: generic wellness branding, sterile minimalism, hard corporate layouts, overly loud neon chaos

## Suggested palette
- Citrus red: `#DE271A`
- Peel orange: `#F28C28`
- Pulp cream: `#F7F1E8`
- Leaf green: `#526B3E`
- Night ink: `#171310`
- Soft blush: `#D9A6A0`

## Typography direction
- Headings: high-contrast serif or editorial-feeling display type
- Body: clean sans-serif
- Overall feeling: art-book meets citrus label

## Layout direction
- Big breathing room
- Strong hero statement
- Asymmetric but controlled sections
- Use color blocks sparingly
- Let images or artwork lead when available

## Content direction
- Focus on identity, product/story, atmosphere, and invitation to connect
- Copy should feel confident and sensual, not salesy
- Short paragraphs, memorable lines, selective emphasis
